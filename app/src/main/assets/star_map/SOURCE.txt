Upstream data provenance documented by d3-celestial:
- stars.6.json: XHIP / Extended Hipparcos Compilation, converted by d3-celestial
- constellations.json and constellations.lines.json: IAU constellation data with name positions
  and line modifications documented by d3-celestial

Mica does not bundle d3-celestial JavaScript. The Android player parses the GeoJSON data and
renders its own native OpenGL ES star-map view.
